# Focus Desk Clock: User Guide

Focus Desk Clock turns a phone or tablet into a desk clock, a focus timer and a stopwatch.
It needs no account and keeps everything on your device.

## Install it on your phone

1. Open **https://focus-desk-clock.netlify.app** in **Chrome**.
2. Let the page load once while you are online.
3. Tap the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
4. Open **Desk Clock** from your home screen. It runs full screen with no browser bars.

After that it also works without internet.

## The three modes

Use the tabs at the top:

- **Clock:** shows the current time.
- **Timer:** counts down.
- **Stopwatch:** counts up.

## Using the timer

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Change** | Switches between Focus (90 min), Short Break (10 min) and Long Break (20 min) |
| **Set** | Pick your own time with the arrows, or use a Quick pick button |
| **Start / Pause / Resume** | Starts, pauses or continues the countdown |
| **Clear** | Stops and resets the timer |

The bottom row shows the total time, the time the session will end, and how many sessions you finished today.

When time is up, an alarm plays and **TIME'S UP** pulses on screen. Tap anywhere to silence it.

## Daily stats

Tap the **bar-chart icon** (top left) to see today's focus sessions, total time focused and breaks.
**Reset today** clears only these numbers, and it asks you to tap twice so you cannot do it by accident.

## Other buttons

- **Corner icon (top right):** full screen on and off.
- **? button:** replays the welcome tour.

## Keeping the screen on

The screen stays awake while the clock or a timer is running. Plug your phone in for long sessions.
The bottom row shows **SCREEN AWAKE ON** when this is working.

## Troubleshooting

- **No sound:** raise the media volume, and tap the page once (browsers require a tap before sound).
- **SCREEN AWAKE says N/A:** your browser does not support it. Use Chrome and the https:// address.
- **Screen still turns off on Xiaomi/Redmi:** go to Settings, Apps, Manage apps, Chrome, Battery saver, and choose **No restrictions**.
- **No Install option:** make sure you opened the https:// link in Chrome, not inside another app.
- **Something looks old after an update:** close the app fully and open it again while online.
